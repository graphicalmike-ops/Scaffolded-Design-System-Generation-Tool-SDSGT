Three tracks, one product team

Pick a lane or roam freely. Every pass opens all three tracks, so designers, product managers and engineers learn side by side.

Track 01 · Product design

Design for people and the agents working beside them

See how leading teams prototype with models, design AI interactions people trust, and turn their design systems into something an agent can build from.

  • Patterns for explaining, correcting and undoing AI actions
  • A token workflow your coding agents actually follow
  • Research methods that keep pace with weekly releases

Explore track

Track 02 · Product management

Plan products on ground that keeps moving

Learn how product leaders set strategy, price AI features and write specs when the underlying models improve every few months.

  • A roadmap format built for model volatility
  • Pricing frameworks for usage-based AI features
  • Specs an agent can build from without guessing

Explore track

Track 03 · Product development

Build with AI pair-builders, ship with confidence

Engineering leads share what really happens when agents join the codebase: the workflows that stuck, the guardrails that saved them, and the evals that catch regressions.

  • Eval suites that catch regressions before users do
  • Guardrails for agents inside your CI pipeline
  • Latency and cost budgets that hold up at scale
